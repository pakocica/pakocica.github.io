Reading
Mas-Colell, Whinston & Green, Microeconomic Theory (MWG), chapters 1, 2, 3 and 6; Muñoz-Garcia, Advanced Microeconomic Theory, chapters 1, 2 and 5. Each class’s sections are linked as PDFs, for students of this course: sign in with your UB e-mail address to open them (the readings for the next class and the last class open without sign-in).
details
  • A. Mas-Colell, M. D. Whinston & J. R. Green, Microeconomic Theory (Oxford University Press, 1995), cited as MWG — the main text. Chapters 1, 2, 3 and 6 for Part I; the session plan gives the sections for each class.
  • F. Muñoz-Garcia, Advanced Microeconomic Theory: An Intuitive Approach with Examples (MIT Press, 2017) — the same material at a gentler pace, with worked examples. Chapters 1, 2 and 5.
Problem sets
Two, in groups of 2–5, on paper at the start of class. Problem set 1 due Wed 14 Oct, problem set 2 due Wed 21 Oct.
details

Two problem sets, done in groups of 2–5, handed in on paper at the start of class. One submission per group, with all names on it. Form your group before Wednesday 30 September, when a sign-up sheet goes round in class.

  • Problem set 1 — preferences and utility. Out Tuesday 6 October, due in class Wednesday 14 October.
  • Problem set 2 — utility maximisation, indirect utility, expenditure minimisation. Out Wednesday 14 October, due in class Wednesday 21 October; returned graded Tuesday 27 October.
Quizzes
Short, on paper, in class, at least one a week. Graded from Wed 30 Sep; the lowest score is dropped.
details

Short quizzes on paper during class, at least one a week, on the reading and on what we did in the previous sessions. Closed book, a few minutes each.

  • The quiz in the first class is not graded. It shows me what you already know.
  • Every quiz after the first one is graded.
  • Your lowest quiz score is dropped. There are no make-ups for a missed quiz.
Sextant
Practice app for this course: pkocourek.com/sextant/?c=micro-1. Not graded.
details

Sextant is a study app I built for this course. For each class there are three short sets of questions: one to prepare, one after class, and an optional one that goes deeper. Questions you miss come back later. It works on a phone or a laptop, needs no account, and nothing in it is graded or reported.

Use of AI
None in quizzes and exams. Allowed in the problem sets if the group declares how it was used.
details

Use of AI: none in quizzes and exams. In the problem sets, allowed if the group declares how it was used, in a few lines at the end. Undeclared use is treated as copying.
