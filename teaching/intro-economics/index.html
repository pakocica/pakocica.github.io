To pass by continuous assessment: at least 40 % in every component above, and at least 50 % overall. Students who pass this way do not sit the final exam.

Otherwise: you take the final exam in January, which counts for the whole grade (not recommended). Missing either midterm also puts you on this route. The date, the format and the re-evaluation arrangements are announced by the course coordinator.

Use of AI: none in quizzes and midterms. Allowed in problem sets and in the project, as long as you declare how you used it — a few lines saying what you asked it to do and what you did with the answer. Undeclared use is treated as copying.
