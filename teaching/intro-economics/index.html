Sextant
Practice app for this course: pkocourek.com/sextant/?c=intro-econ. Not graded.
details

Sextant is a study app I built for this course. Each class has a short set of questions on the assigned reading and on what we did in class, and the reading itself is in the app, read aloud section by section. Questions you miss come back later. It works on a phone or a laptop, needs no account to start, and nothing in it is graded or reported. Signing in with your UB e-mail address is recommended, though: it unlocks some additional functionality.

Use of AI
None in quizzes and midterms. Allowed in problem sets and the project if you declare how you used it.
details

Use of AI: none in quizzes and midterms. Allowed in problem sets and in the project, as long as you declare how you used it — a few lines saying what you asked it to do and what you did with the answer. Undeclared use is treated as copying.
