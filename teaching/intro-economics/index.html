To pass by continuous assessment: a midterm average of at least 5/10 with at least 4/10 in each midterm, and at least 40 % in the problem sets and in the project. There is no minimum for the in-class quizzes. Students who pass this way do not sit the final exam.

Otherwise: the final exam on 13 January 2027 (12:00–15:00) counts 100 %: 20 % multiple choice (minimum 3.5/10 in that section), 40 % theory, 40 % problems. Missing either midterm means taking this route. Re-evaluation: 3 February 2027; continuous-assessment marks are not carried over.

Use of AI: none in quizzes and midterms; allowed in problem sets if you say where you used it; allowed in the project if you hand in the conversation log with a note on where the AI was wrong or unsupported.
